About

My research focuses on the economics of education, public economics, and labor economics — with an emphasis on how regulation and institutions shape teacher careers and student trajectories through school systems in Chile.

I hold an MS in Economics (2025) and a BS in Industrial Engineering (2023), both from Universidad de Chile. I've worked as a research assistant with the Millennium Initiative for Labor Market Mismatch, PUC-Chile, Brunel University London, the Center for Advanced Research in Education (U. de Chile), and the Central Bank of Chile. I've presented work at the KU Leuven Workshop on the Economics of Education and AERA.

Languages: Spanish (native), English (fluent), Portuguese (intermediate–advanced).
